Privacy Policy

NexaHub is designed to minimize data collection in its static, browser-based experience.

Local processing

Tools that are implemented client-side process their inputs in your browser. Images selected for image tools are processed locally and are not uploaded by NexaHub.

Local storage

Your browser may store favorites, recently used tools and your theme preference using localStorage. This information stays in the browser profile unless you clear it or the site changes it.

External links

Some links, such as the GitHub link in the footer, lead to third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Contact

For privacy questions or to report a problem, email mehrablone300@gmail.com.

Future features

If NexaHub later adds API-powered or cloud features, this policy should be updated before those features collect or transmit information.
